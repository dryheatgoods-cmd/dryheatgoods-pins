Privacy Policy

Dry Heat Goods Co · Effective September 24, 2026

Dry Heat Goods Co is a small print-on-demand apparel shop based in Phoenix, Arizona. We sell original T-shirt designs through our Etsy shop and share them on Pinterest. This policy explains how our website and our Pinterest integration handle information.

Our Pinterest integration

We use the Pinterest API only to manage our own Pinterest business account. Our app creates and schedules Pins that show our own product designs, organizes them on our own boards, and reads basic performance statistics (such as impressions, saves and clicks) for those Pins so we can see which designs people like.

The app is used only by the shop owner. It does not ask other Pinterest users to log in, and it does not collect, store or process personal information about other Pinterest users.

Information we store

  • An access token for our own Pinterest account, stored securely on the shop owner's computer and used only to post to and read statistics from that account.
  • Aggregate performance numbers for our own Pins, stored on the shop owner's computer.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. We do not use it for advertising profiles.

This website

This website is a simple information page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on it, and we do not collect any information from visitors. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

All purchases happen on Etsy. Order and payment information is handled by Etsy under Etsy's Privacy Policy. Orders are fulfilled by our production partner, Printify, which receives the shipping details needed to print and ship each order.

Deleting data

We can revoke our app's access to our Pinterest account and delete the stored token and statistics at any time. If you have a question about your information, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
